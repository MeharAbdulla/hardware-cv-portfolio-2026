# Upwork-safe sharing notes

This repository is a **personal portfolio** of hardware and computer-vision prototypes built roughly **3 Aug – 2 Sep 2026**.

## Safe to share

- Link this public repo on your Upwork profile.
- Share individual project folders with prospective clients.
- Photos and demo frames that do not show personal accounts, SSIDs, or private dashboards.

## Do not commit or share

- Real `.env` / `secrets.h` with Wi‑Fi passwords, MQTT tokens, Home Assistant tokens, or API keys.
- Client-branded deliverables or NDA-covered code (keep those in private repos).
- Full-length large demo videos (keep locally; offer on request).

## Client work workflow

1. New paid work starts in a **private** repo.
2. Invite the client as a collaborator.
3. Only publish a sanitized demo after written approval.
